✦Lyss AI Assistant AI AGENT

A document workflow helper that turns requests into checked, share-ready results.

In one sentence: Lyss receives a document task, follows a clear review workflow, and returns an organized result with open questions visible.

💡 What it is

Lyss AI Assistant is a personal AI Agent in Lark. It helps with document-focused requests such as checking a template, organizing supplied content, and explaining what changed.

It supports your work and leaves final review and sharing decisions to you.

⏱ What work it helps with

Manual reviewRead the request, inspect the document, check required fields, and write a status update.
With LyssAsk the Agent to inspect specified parts, summarize the result, and flag anything unclear.

Time saved depends on the document and task; the Agent does not claim a fixed saving.

🧰 Tools in this workflow

Lark MessengerReceive the task and clarify the user’s request.
Lark Docs / filesReview supplied documents and prepare structured content.
Lyss AI AssistantOrganize the work, check requested details, and report the result.
Human reviewConfirm accuracy and approve any sharing or external action.

⚙ Standard workflow

Closed loop: receive → understand → verify inputs → process → check boundaries → output → close.

1

Receive the task

Capture the task, context, source document, and requested format.

2

Understand and classify

Check whether the task fits document support and identify missing details.

3

Collect information

Use only the provided document and tools available in the authorized workspace.

4

Process the request

Review, organize, or format the requested content while preserving required fields.

5

Check limits and risks

Flag missing, conflicting, or uncertain information; ask before external sharing.

6

Produce standard output

Return the result, what was checked, assumptions, and next action.

7

Close the task

Confirm completion and provide the document or file location when available.

📤 Outputs

Result summaryWhat was completed and what was checked.
Structured contentOrganized text or a document, when supported.
Open questionsMissing inputs and assumptions that need review.
Next actionA clear handoff or a shareable location, if created.

✅ What Lyss can do / ⛔ What it cannot do

✓ Can

  • Organize document tasks and supplied information
  • Check named fields or sections
  • Summarize results and uncertainty

× Cannot

  • Verify content it cannot access
  • Invent missing facts or claim certainty without evidence
  • Decide who receives a document without instruction

🚀 How to use

In Lark, open a chat with Lyss AI Assistant, attach or link the document, and state exactly what to check.

Review the attached document. Check that [fields/sections] are present and intact. Summarize what you found, list anything missing or uncertain, and ask before sharing or changing access.

⚠ Notes

  • Review the Agent’s result before relying on it.
  • Access to a source document depends on your Lark permissions.
  • Public links and external sharing require the user’s decision.
